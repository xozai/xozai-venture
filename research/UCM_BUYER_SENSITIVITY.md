# The unknown nobody sensitivity-tested

Researcher, closing the D dispute-quote pass, said something that matters more than
that pass did:

> "I scored civil buyer=user=5 on the individual-PE-with-a-card read from job
> postings, not from any quote — worth being explicit that assumption hasn't been
> quote-verified either."

Correcting the attribution first: **buyer = user is my dimension and that 5 is my
score.** Researcher supplied the job-posting evidence; reading it as a buyer is
mine, and so is this correction.

## The conflation

The scan's UCM entry reads:

> **Budget holder:** individual PE/EIT at a private transportation or land-development
> design firm. Job-posting evidence: EIT duties explicitly include "identifying utility
> conflicts," "managing the UCM as a living document"…

Job postings prove the EIT **does the work**. They prove nothing about who **buys**.
I scored a purchasing assumption off task-ownership evidence and did not mark the
step.

This is the same shape as the named-pain trap already recorded from the OPCC pick:
a documented workflow read as evidence of something it does not evidence. Last time
it was "we described the pain ourselves." This time it is "we described the worker
and called them the buyer."

## What it costs, and why it is bigger than the unknown that *was* tested

The scan sensitivity-tested named pain (UCM 76–91) and concluded:

> "Both stay in the top two bands across the whole range and **their relative order
> does not change**."

That claim is true **for the unknown it tested**. Against this one it does not hold.

| Scenario | Buyer | Refs | Raw | **Norm** | Band |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — private-firm PE buys (as scored)** | 5 | 4 | 64 | **80** | 5 |
| S3b — mild softening | 4 | 3 | 60 | **75** | 4 |
| S3 — hybrid: consultant champions, agency pays | 3 | 2 | 56 | **70** | 4 |
| S2 — public agency is the buyer | 1 | 1 | 49 | **61.25** | 3 |

Reference velocity moves with the buyer, not independently: the rubric's 1-anchor is
literally "public agencies that cannot endorse vendors." Channel fit is held at 5
in every row — APWA and UESI reach public-works engineers as well as consultants, and
PDH credit works on agency PEs too. That is deliberately conservative against my own
argument; the drop below is not manufactured by pessimistic channel scoring.

**TIS scores 75.** So UCM's lead is 5 normalized points, and:

- S3b — a *one-point* softening of the buyer assumption — erases it exactly.
- S3 and S2 reverse the order.

**UCM outranks TIS only if the private-firm-buyer assumption holds at full strength.**
The scan attributes UCM's win to liability drag, which is right about the mechanism
but incomplete about the margin: the liability advantage (+6 raw) is nearly cancelled
by UCM's weaker time-to-obvious (−3), leaving a 4-raw-point lead that the buyer
assumption is carrying.

## The condition this depends on — stated, not assumed

None of the above matters unless **UCM's buyer assumption is more fragile than TIS's.**
If both fail together, the order is unchanged and this is noise.

My reasoning for the asymmetry, offered as reasoning and not as sourced fact:

- A **TIS** is produced for an applicant seeking entitlement and *submitted to* an
  agency for approval. The agency is the reviewer; the private applicant pays. The
  private-buyer read is structurally sound.
- **UCM** work is centred on agency-owned capital projects. Every quote in
  `UCM_PAIN_QUOTES.md` is a county or city engineer on a public road project. The
  consultant maintaining the matrix is working under an agency contract, and software
  bought to do contracted work is frequently specified — or reimbursed — by the agency.

So the fragility is plausibly asymmetric, in the direction that hurts UCM specifically.
**This needs confirming, and it is not confirmed.**

## What settles it

The same single conversation that would close gate item 3. One call with a PE or EIT
at a transportation design firm answers both:

1. Do you describe *maintaining the matrix* as painful, in your own words? (gate item 3)
2. **Would your firm buy a tool for this on its own card, or would it be specified or
   reimbursed through the agency contract?** (this dimension)

Question 2 costs nothing extra to ask and is worth more to the ranking than question 1.
Whoever does that outreach should ask both.

## What I am not doing

**I am not rescoring UCM.** S1 remains the scored value at 80 — the job-posting evidence
for who does the work is real, and I have no evidence for S2 or S3 beyond an inference
from who happens to get quoted in local news, a confound I already flagged. Rescoring on
a hypothesis would repeat the error this file exists to correct.

What changes is the **confidence statement attached to the score**, and one line in the
scan that is now too strong. For @Hermes's ranking memo:

> UCM at 80 rests on an unverified purchasing assumption. Softening that assumption by
> one point on a five-point scale erases its lead over TIS entirely. The ranking is
> robust to the named-pain unknown, as the scan says; it is **not** robust to this one.

If joseleos wants the pick made before that call happens, that is a legitimate call to
make with the uncertainty priced in — but it should be made knowing the two leads are
inside each other's error bars, not five points apart.
