# Space profile: enterprise sales performance management (SPM)

**Scope (joseleos, 2026-08-30):** an AI-forward software solution in enterprise SPM —
the systems and workflows around sales compensation, quotas, territories, and
sales-performance analytics. "AI-forward" is a constraint: the wedge must be
something LLMs/agents make newly possible or 10× cheaper, not a re-skin of an
incumbent module.

**Sub-areas to sweep (target 8–12 candidates):**
incentive-comp plan design and modeling; plan-document drafting and legal review;
quota setting and capacity planning; territory design and account assignment;
commission calculation and statement generation; dispute/inquiry handling
("why is my commission X?"); SPIF and contest management; accrual/finance close
and ASC 606 commission capitalization; clawback and adjustment workflows;
rep-facing earnings forecasting; comp-plan communication and rollout; analytics
and pay-for-performance diagnostics; CRM/CPQ/ERP data reconciliation.

**Buyer archetypes:** sales comp analyst / manager (user, budget via RevOps);
RevOps / Sales Ops leader; CRO; CFO / controller (accuracy, audit); HR total
rewards. Card-purchasable buyer is rare — expect sales-assisted; note this on the
buyer = user dimension honestly rather than forcing a self-serve story.

**Named incumbents (price against them):** Xactly, Varicent, CaptivateIQ, Spiff
(Salesforce), Anaplan, SAP Commissions (Callidus), Oracle SPM, QuotaPath,
Everstage, Forma.ai, Performio, Palette. Capture public pricing where it exists.

**Liability analog for the rubric ("stamped deliverable"):** does the output
determine money paid to employees or booked in financials (payroll, ASC 606,
audit)? yes / no / assists. Errors there are lawsuits and restatements, not rework.

**Where unprompted complaints live:** r/sales, r/salesoperations, RevOps Co-op
Slack, Pavilion, LinkedIn posts by comp analysts, G2/Capterra 1–3★ reviews of the
incumbents, WorldatWork sales-comp forums, job postings for "sales compensation
analyst" (duties = budgeted manual work).

**Channels:** RevOps Co-op / Pavilion communities, WorldatWork Sales Comp
conference, SaaStr, LinkedIn thought-leadership, G2 category listings,
partnerships with CRM/CPQ consultancies, CFO/controller newsletters.

**Scoring:** use `research/SPM_RUBRIC_ADDENDUM.md` (Scribe), not the civil
anchors. "Buyer = user" becomes "buying-committee friction."

**SPM-only required capture (feeds wedge narrative):** *one concrete sentence on
what the AI does here that an incumbent cannot also claim.* All named incumbents
already market "AI-powered," so that phrase is noise; if the honest answer is
"nothing specific," the candidate caps at 3 on wedge.

**Directional hint:** low-liability ground is the design / modeling / diagnostic /
communication layer (plan design, dispute handling, comp communication) — not the
calculation engine, which is both the contested mid-market ground and where a
wrong number becomes a wage claim.
